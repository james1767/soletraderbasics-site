# Free Bookkeeping Software for Sole Traders UK (2026)

## Quick answer

Free bookkeeping software for sole traders can be enough if you need basic income tracking, expense tracking, receipt storage, and simple invoices.

The best free option depends on what you already use. Some sole traders use a spreadsheet. Others use a free bookkeeping app, a business bank account with built-in tools, or accounting software included with a bank account.

If you need full accounting features, compare the options in [best accounting software for sole traders](/best/accounting-software-sole-trader/).

## What free bookkeeping software should do

At minimum, a useful free bookkeeping tool should help you:

- record income
- record business expenses
- store receipts
- send or track invoices
- categorise transactions
- export records for your tax return or accountant

It does not need to do everything on day one. The right tool is the one you will actually keep updated.

## Free options for sole traders

### Spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is the simplest free bookkeeping option.

It can work well if you have low transaction volume and do not mind entering figures manually. Use separate tabs for income, expenses, mileage, and yearly totals.

Start here: [bookkeeping spreadsheet template](/guides/accounting-software/bookkeeping-spreadsheet-template/).

### Business bank account tools

Some business bank accounts include basic features such as spending categories, tax pots, invoices, or exports.

This can be useful if you want bookkeeping to start from your bank account rather than a separate system. Compare options in [best free business bank accounts for sole traders](/guides/banking/best-free-business-bank-account-sole-trader/).

### Free accounting software via a bank account

Some UK banking setups have included accounting software access for eligible customers. Availability and terms can change, so always check the provider before relying on it.

This can be a strong option if you want both a separate business account and proper bookkeeping software without an extra monthly software bill.

### Free trials and starter plans

Some accounting software providers offer free trials or low-cost starter plans. These are not always free long term, but they can help you test whether software is worth paying for.

Before starting a trial, check:

- monthly price after the trial
- whether bank feeds are included
- invoice limits
- receipt capture features
- export options if you leave

## When free bookkeeping software is enough

Free tools may be enough if:

- your business is new
- you have a small number of transactions
- you do not need advanced reports
- you are not VAT registered
- you are happy to do some manual checking

They can be a good first step while you learn how your income and expenses behave.

## When to compare paid accounting software

Consider paid or full-featured accounting software if:

- you send regular invoices
- you have lots of expenses
- you want automatic bank feeds
- you want receipt capture
- you work with an accountant
- your records are taking too long to maintain
- you want a clearer view of profit and tax estimates

If that sounds like you, read [best accounting software for sole traders](/best/accounting-software-sole-trader/). That comparison is the better place to choose between full bookkeeping and accounting tools.

## Free does not mean no admin

Free software still needs accurate information.

You will still usually need to:

- check imported transactions
- keep receipts
- categorise expenses carefully
- record cash payments
- keep records for the required period

For the basics, read [sole trader record keeping requirements](/guides/accounting-software/sole-trader-record-keeping-requirements/).

## Final recommendation

If you are a beginner, start with the simplest free system you can keep up with.

Use a spreadsheet if your business is very small. Use bank account tools if you want simple categories and separation. Compare full accounting software when you want automation, invoices, bank feeds, and less manual bookkeeping.

## FAQ

### Is there free bookkeeping software for sole traders?

Yes. Some sole traders use spreadsheets, bank account tools, free starter plans, or accounting software included with certain business bank accounts.

### Is free accounting software enough?

It can be enough for a simple sole trader with few transactions. Full accounting software may be easier if you invoice regularly, have many expenses, or want automatic bank feeds.

### What is the best bookkeeping app for a sole trader?

The best app depends on your business, transaction volume, and whether you want bank feeds, invoicing, receipt capture, or Self Assessment support.
