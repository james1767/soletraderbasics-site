# Best Free Business Bank Accounts for Sole Traders UK

## Quick answer

The best free business bank account for a sole trader is usually one that has no monthly fee, easy payments, a clear app, and useful bookkeeping features. Starling, Mettle, Tide, and Monzo Business Lite are common options for UK sole traders.

Sole traders are not legally required to have a separate business bank account, but a separate account is often useful. If you are ready for [setting up a sole trader business bank account](/guides/banking/do-i-need-business-bank-account-sole-trader/), start with a free account that keeps admin simple.

## Main explanation

A free business bank account can help you separate business income from personal spending without adding another monthly bill.

Look for:

- No monthly account fee
- Free or low-cost UK bank transfers
- A debit card for business spending
- Simple app controls
- Accounting software links if you use bookkeeping software
- Clear fees for cash deposits, international payments, or extra transfers

Free does not always mean every feature is free. Some providers charge for cash deposits, extra transfers, invoicing tools, or paid plan upgrades. Check the fees that match how you actually work.

## Practical examples

If most clients pay by bank transfer and you rarely handle cash, a digital account may be enough. You can receive payments, pay expenses, and keep your records separate.

If you want accounting software included, Mettle may be worth comparing because it has offered FreeAgent access for eligible users.

If you want a strong all-round app with simple banking features, Starling is often a sensible place to start.

If you send lots of invoices or want built-in business tools, compare Tide's free and paid plans carefully so you know when fees start.

If you already use Monzo personally, Monzo Business Lite may feel familiar, but check whether you need features from its paid plan.

## Final recommendation

Choose the free account that matches your transactions, not the one with the longest feature list. For many sole traders, the best first step is a simple free account used only for business income and business expenses.

Once it is open, use it consistently. That habit matters more than picking a perfect bank on day one. The next step is usually a simple [sole trader bookkeeping system](/guides/accounting-software/sole-trader-bookkeeping/) for income, expenses, and receipts.

## FAQ

### Do sole traders need to pay for a business bank account?

No. Many sole traders use free business bank accounts. Check fees carefully, because some services charge for specific actions even when there is no monthly fee.

### What should I look for in a free account?

Look for no monthly fee, easy transfers, a debit card, clear transaction fees, and accounting software links if you need them.

### Is a free business account enough?

Often, yes. A free account is usually enough for basic sole trader banking if you mainly receive payments, pay expenses, and want cleaner records.
